Profile Data

SPLIT stores creator profile details, contact information, consent timestamps, and split-sheet data in Supabase-backed tables protected by row-level security.

Authentication

Email, password registration, login, and password recovery are handled through Supabase Auth. SPLIT does not store passwords in custom database tables.

Collaborator Matching

SPLIT may use usernames, email addresses, or phone numbers to match collaborators to pending split sheets and route invitations to the correct account.
