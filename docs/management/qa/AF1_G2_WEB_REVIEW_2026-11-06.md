## What was independently checked

This does not show that Swift/JS decoders reject the negatives; the future raw lexical gate must catch `hex` versus `h\u0065x`, exponent/fraction/negative zero, invalid UTF-8, unpaired escapes and null-versus-omission *before* lossy typed decoding.
- **Byte and context correspondence.** Recomputed all 13 table preimage lengths/SHA-256 values, plus the forged body and backing array. The 27-byte backing string's offset-4, length-18 subview exactly equals P1-executed-1; its full-buffer digest is different. P1/r1 has one executed entry followed by completion; P2/r1 has two executed entries in prepared document order followed by one completion; P1/r2 preserves roles, IDs, order, lengths and raw bodies. Only retrieval time and reconciliation ID change on each r2 entry. Repeated executed roles in P2 are required, not a duplicate-role error.
- **Pairing.** Checked prepared/terms/signer/document-set digest objects against their independently hashed table records; exact ReadRef-to-projection rename; manifest execution/frozen/attempt; byte-metadata entry, manifest revision and lengths; optional/pinned lookup and byte request; all six failure examples. A/B base projections differ only in ownParty, accessRevision and viewTag. The manifest object is not personalized, but the replies and read authorization are. B/r2 and readiness-driver values derived by the textual deltas are not falsely counted as additional fenced records.
- **Immutable version selection.** P1 uses one stable manifest ID. Revision 1 binds projection 10; approved revision 2 binds projection 11 and a new viewTag. A higher number supplied by a response is not a trusted revision. A rollback cannot replace current results; an equal-revision, different manifest under the same binding is an integrity conflict. A later stale invocation must be discarded before it can quarantine a newer result. Compare projection bodies within the same authorized account/view context, excluding diagnostic requestId and normalizing only the permit set; do not compare A's and B's personalized projections as a global immutable body. A fresh trusted access/view change invalidates old loads, not the immutable historical manifest.
- **Whole-set barrier.** Stage owned bytes privately. Each specific byte read must have the current view, full frozen/attempt, manifest version and entry grant. After the final certificate, the initial and final manifest calls must each check both logical permits and *all* grants in a consistent registry observation, before and after a hold. The final reread must match the pinned set; then compare-and-publish locally without another suspension. A new denial, observer generation, account/selection ABA, manifest advance, or more recent explicit load wins over old success **and old errors**. Stale work cannot clear newer bytes, advance the denial epoch, mutate high-water marks or trigger recovery. This does not establish continuous detection of a remote change after the last observed check.
- **Selection and outcomes.** Current/current and expressly authorized history/superseded may become integrity-validated only with the complete set. Current/superseded and history/current are stale; superseding is not eligible under either intent. Denial beats existence, stale/readiness and detailed integrity diagnostics for unauthorized resources. A malformed *reply* for the still-current owner is not an authorized stale *request*. The stale, samplePending, unavailable cases permit only an explicit fresh projection read; denied, integrity and unsupportedFixture have no automatic recovery. S2's duplicate-session 409/receipt is neither an AF1 failure nor an artifact read credential.

## Acceptance notes for a future handoff

1. **Keep the G1 AF-07 coverage boundary explicit.** G2:294-295's P1-Assembling starts with simulatedPending, pending parties and two unresolved recipients. It is a useful pending control, *not* the distinct “simulatedComplete, all parties uniquely signed, zero unresolved, files still assembling” positive. The rules give the latter no eligible bytes and samplePending for an authorized current read. Do not claim that vector was demonstrated. A later claim covering the full G1 case needs an independently seeded canonical driver; changing an untrusted response on the fly is not a positive control. This is not a blocker to implementing precisely the G2 vectors.
2. **Keep world and identity keys intact.** The AF1 P2 sheet UUID is also used by GM13E for legacy L. They belong to different isolated fixture worlds/contracts. Do not key any cross-world grant, cached result or fallback solely by sheet UUID. Similarly P1-H/P2 show historical/current selection isolation, not real same-sheet supersession, replacement dispatch, or a verified E1→E2 relationship.
3. **Make the stale-notice assertion discriminate.** In addition to an obsolete observer instance, cover a previously true revocation held through a regrant/new positive. Prove it is obsolete by trusted current generation/context or current registry observation, not merely by its late arrival. Also retain a genuinely current post-completion denial positive. Do not advance local epochs for discarded work or grant new eligibility merely from a notice.
4. **Independent QA remains necessary.** Use real current-owner operations and deterministic holds for stale successes, typed failures, thrown errors, revoke-first-while-reading-certificate, r1/r2 and store retirement. Check unsafe omissions using independent expected values. Never transform an integrity-only result into a viewer result with a flag. No owner, independent runtime, HTTP, PDF parser, OS/device, database, provider, legal or release evidence has been produced by this review.
